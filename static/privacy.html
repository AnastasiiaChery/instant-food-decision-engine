Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

This policy explains what personal data NomPilot ("we", "the service") collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

1. Who is responsible (data controller)

NomPilot is an independent, non-commercial project operated by an individual ("we"). For any privacy question, to exercise your rights, or to request the controller's full identity, contact us at nompilot.service@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, why, and the legal basis

DataPurposeLegal basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Your location (GPS coordinates, or an address/map point you enter) To find food and drink venues near you — the core function of the service. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), asked before location is first used.
Account data — email address, display name, and (for email sign-up) a hashed password; or your Google account ID, email and name if you sign in with Google To create and secure your account and keep you signed in. Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
Search & taste preferences — your query text, group/occasion, and the diet, cuisine and drink preferences you choose to set To personalise recommendations to your taste. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Some diet choices (e.g. halal, kosher) may reveal religious beliefs — a special category under Art. 9; we process them only with your explicit consent and only to filter recommendations.
History — places you navigate to or favourite To show your recent activity and improve suggestions. Your consent / legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
Feedback you submit (and your email, if signed in) To understand problems and improve the service during beta. Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
Usage analytics — anonymous, first-party events (page views, searches, which mode you use, navigate/favourite clicks) tied to a random identifier stored in your browser, plus basic request logs (URL path, response time, errors). No cookies, no third-party tracker, no cross-site tracking. To understand how many people use the service and where it works or fails, so we can improve it. Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). The data stays on our own servers and is not used to identify you.

3. Who we share data with (recipients)

We do not sell your data. To provide the service we send the minimum necessary data to:

  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API — we send your coordinates to fetch nearby venues. (openstreetmap.org)
  • Groq — we send your query and taste preferences (not your account email) to an AI model that ranks places and translates the interface. Groq may process this outside the EEA; see Section 4. (groq.com)
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google", for authentication. (Google's own privacy policy applies.)
  • Telegram — if you submit feedback, your message (and email, if signed in) is delivered to our team via Telegram.

4. International transfers

Some processors (e.g. Groq, Google) may process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated in their data-processing terms. You can ask us for more detail at any time.

5. How long we keep data

  • Location used for a search is processed transiently to return results and is not stored as raw coordinates tied to your account beyond what is needed to show your history.
  • Account, preferences and history are kept while your account exists. Delete your account and we erase them within [30] days, except where law requires retention.
  • Cached translations and venue data are technical caches with a short lifetime and contain no account identifiers.
  • Usage analytics are kept for up to 90 days (behaviour events) and 30 days (request logs), then automatically deleted.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can: access your data; correct it; erase it ("right to be forgotten"); restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect processing already carried out). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, email nompilot.service@gmail.com.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; we will revise the "last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
